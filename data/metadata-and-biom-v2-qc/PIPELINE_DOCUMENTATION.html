4. Current QC Rules

Step 1. Merge: metadata_all_for_qc_raw.tsv: 116,643 samples, 334 studies, 45,880 subjects. Oral raw subset: 86,255 samples, 334 studies, 44,326 subjects.
Step 2. Study-level filter: Remove a study if ASV <= 30 in >50% samples, chimera >25% in >30% samples, or closed-reference retention <50%. Current result: 47 studies removed.
Step 3. Sample-level ASV filter: Remove remaining samples with asv_count <= 30.
Step 4. Completeness filter: Remove remaining samples with Observed == NA or subject_id == NA.
Step 5. Oral subset: Select site_group == 'oral'.
Step 6. Oral deduplication: Keep one sample per subject_id x site_cleaned pair.
